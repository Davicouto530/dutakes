Hi, I'm DuTakes.

Whether it's a wedding or a photo shoot, Dutakes skillfully combines technique and sensitivity to create images that tell stories and evoke emotion.
With a unique style and meticulous attention to detail, Dutakes is the perfect choice for those seeking quality, professionalism, and a touch of magic in every click!

Let's talk

Dutakes' journey began in 2023, when a passion for photography took over. Without formal training, relying solely on dedication and curiosity, our founder explored techniques independently and gained his first clients.

During that early period, he balanced photography with his job at a company, until the art of capturing stories became an essential part of his life.

This journey of effort and overcoming challenges reflects Dutakes' purpose: to show that every moment deserves to be immortalized with authenticity, emotion, and professionalism.
